  INTRODUCTION TO BRAIN TUMOR DETECTION

P176 [cfr_Chaptercontent] (align=None) [Runs: default/12.0/]:
  One of the most dangerous neurological conditions affecting the human brain is brain tumors. When aberrant cells proliferate uncontrolled inside brain tissues, it can become a brain tumor, which can interfere with regular brain activities and, if left undetected, might be fatal. In order to improve treatment results and raise patient survival rates, early and correct diagnosis is essential.

P177 [cfr_Chaptercontent] (align=None) [Runs: default/12.0/,default/12.0/,default/12.0/]:
  Doctors frequently utilize medical imaging technologies like Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) to look at brain architecture and find anomalies. Medical practitioners can determine the existence, size and location of tumors by using the comprehensive pictures of the brain that MRI scans reveal. However, it takes a lot of effort and experience to manually analyze these photographs. Manual analysis frequently results in errors or delays in diagnosis, particularly when a lot of photos need to be reviewed.

P178 [cfr_Chaptercontent] (align=None) [Runs: default/12.0/]:
  Automated systems can now help medical personnel analyze medical pictures more effectively because to the quick development of deep learning and artificial intelligence technology. Large amounts of MRI data may be processed by these clever programs, which might find patterns that would be hard for people to recognize. Consequently, automated brain tumor prediction systems have grown in importance as a field of study in medical image analysis.

P181 [cfr_Chaptercontent] (align=None) [Runs: default/12.0/,default/12.0/,default/12.0/]:

In order to interpret MRI pictures, traditional diagnostic techniques mostly rely on the expertise of radiologists and neurologists. Even though these professionals are essential to the diagnostic process, manually reviewing photographs may be laborious and occasionally prone to human mistake.Medical practitioners can discover cancers more rapidly and precisely with the use of automated brain tumor prediction tools. These algorithms are able to analyze MRI scans, identify aberrant areas and accurately categorize different types of tumors by employing sophisticated deep learning techniques. This increases the consistency and dependability of diagnostic outcomes while also lessening the burden of medical professionals.

  MULTI-TASK MODEL FOR GLIOMA SEPARATION AND CLASSIFICATION BASED ON TRANSFORMERS

P225 [Normal (Web)] (align=JUSTIFY (3)) [Runs: Times New Roman/default/,Times New Roman/default/,Times New Roman/default/]:
  A transformer-based multi-task deep learning model for glioma segmentation and tumor classification using brain MRI images was introduced by Y. Li et.al., in [1]. The model utilizes transformer layers to capture long-range dependencies between image regions, enabling better understanding of complex medical image structures. The architecture allows the network to perform segmentation and classification tasks simultaneously, which improves computational efficiency and feature sharing between tasks. The authors evaluated the model using MRI datasets and reported improved accuracy compared with traditional convolution-based methods. An important contribution of this work is the integration of attention-based learning for medical image analysis. However, the model requires large training datasets and significant computational resources, which may limit its practical use in smaller medical facilities.
